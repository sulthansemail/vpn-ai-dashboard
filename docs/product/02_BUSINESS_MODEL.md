# Business Model

This platform is not a customer-facing SaaS product or a CRM replacement.

It is an internal Operations Platform built for the COO and operations teams.

## Primary value

- Provide an operational layer above the CRM
- Aggregate business and infrastructure data for decision support
- Surface AI-assisted recommendations to improve operational outcomes
- Reduce manual context switching between systems

## What the platform owns

- Mission Control
- Business Intelligence
- Operations Center
- Infrastructure Monitoring
- AI Recommendations
- Capacity Forecasting
- Operational Analytics

## What the CRM owns

- Users
- Payments
- Subscriptions
- Referrals
- Server inventory (transactional source)

## Business model assumptions

- Internal platform for the organization, not a product sold to external customers
- ROI is measured by operational efficiency, incident response speed, and decision accuracy
- The platform increases value by consolidating data and applying AI-driven insights

## Success outcomes

- Faster incident triage and resolution
- Improved forecasting of capacity and churn
- Better alignment between infrastructure and business trends
- Clear, prioritized actions for the Ops team each day
