# Users and Roles

This platform is designed for internal operations and executive use.

## Primary user

- Chief Operating Officer (COO)
  - Uses Mission Control to understand business health, infrastructure risk, and AI recommendations.
  - Prioritizes actions and approves operational programs.

## Secondary users

- Operations team
  - Monitors active alerts and infrastructure health.
  - Executes tasks and follows daily workflows.

- Customer support
  - Uses customer health signals to prioritize support cases.
  - Views churn risk and renewal issues.

- Finance and revenue operations
  - Tracks revenue trends and subscription performance.
  - Validates forecasted capacity and growth assumptions.

- Engineering and infrastructure teams
  - Responds to server incidents, deployments, and capacity recommendations.
  - Uses underlying metrics and AI insights to plan upgrades.

## Role responsibilities

- COO: mission control, strategy, business health review, key action approval.
- Operations lead: incident triage, task assignment, process oversight.
- Support lead: customer health, ticket trends, subscription issues.
- Engineering lead: infrastructure health, deployments, monitoring quality.
- Data/AI engineer: model outputs, data quality, and pipeline integrity.
