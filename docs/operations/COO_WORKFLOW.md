# COO Workflow

Mission Control should drive the daily operational workflow for the COO and operations team.

## Example Daily Review

- 08:00 — Open Mission Control
- Review Critical Alerts
- Review Revenue and Subscriber trends
- Review Infrastructure health and capacity
- Review Customer health and churn indicators
- Review AI recommendations and forecast changes
- Assign tasks and approve actions
- Close daily review with a prioritized task list

## Workflow goals

- Start with business health, then infrastructure.
- Surface the highest-risk issues first.
- Use AI recommendations to decide where to act.
- Turn insight into concrete operational tasks.
