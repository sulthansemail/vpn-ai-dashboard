# Decision Engine

The Decision Engine is the platform’s logic layer for recommending what Operations should do today.

## Purpose

- Turn operational data into prioritized actions
- Combine business health, infrastructure health, and AI signals
- Produce advisory recommendations for the COO and Ops team

## Inputs

- Business trends and revenue signals
- Server health and capacity metrics
- Subscription and churn risk data
- Active alerts and incident state
- Historical performance and forecasts

## Outputs

- Prioritized tasks
- AI recommendations
- Risk scores and severity labels
- Suggested capacity and deployment actions
- Narrative summaries for decision support

## Principles

- The platform recommends, it does not execute directly on the CRM.
- Every recommendation is advisory until approved by Operations.
- The Decision Engine is governed by data quality and operational context.
